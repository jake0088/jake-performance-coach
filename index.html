Privacy Policy

Jake Performance Coach - Last updated: September 1, 2026

Jake Performance Coach is a private application used for personal health, fitness, recovery, sleep, and performance analysis.

Data accessed

With the user's authorization, the application may access WHOOP data including recovery information, heart rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate, sleep data, physiological cycles, workouts, strain, and body measurements.

Purpose

The data is used solely to provide personal fitness, recovery, sleep, training, and performance analysis to the authorized user.

Storage and security

Authorized data may be securely stored in a private database for historical analysis and trend evaluation. Authentication credentials and tokens are not intentionally exposed publicly.

Sharing of data

Personal WHOOP data is not sold or used for advertising. Data may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the application and provide the requested analysis.

Authorization and deletion

WHOOP authorization can be revoked by the user. Stored personal data may also be deleted upon request.

Contact

Questions regarding this privacy policy can be directed to the contact email associated with the Jake Performance Coach application.
